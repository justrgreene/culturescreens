Why we’re here — 01

To
turn on/off
screen culture.

Culture on Screens is an exploration of our lives lived in/through screens. We want to deeply engage questions about representation in cinema, television, games, and Internet artifacts.

Our intent is to create a space for critical reflection on the images and narratives that shape our digital experience.

Part notebook, part conversation starter, this is a place to slow down and pay attention. We believe curiosity can be rigorous, criticism can be generous, and looking closely is a way of being in the world.

01 / THE IMAGE

Pictures don’t just show us something. They tell us how—and where—to look.

LOOK
AGAIN01

Essay / 04 minute read — 01

Every image
has a point of view.

To make an image is to make a choice: this, not that; here, not there. The frame is a boundary, and also an invitation.

We scroll through thousands of pictures in a day. Some stop us. Some slip by. And some quietly become the way we picture a place, a person, a moment in history. Looking critically doesn’t mean looking cynically—it means noticing the choices behind the view.

Who made this? Who is it for? What falls beyond its edges? These questions don’t close a picture down. They let us step inside it with our eyes open.

“To look is to choose a point of view.”FIELD NOTE / 001

Try a second look

Pause on an image that feels familiar. Find the edges. Follow the light. Imagine the scene just outside the frame. A picture can stay the same while your way of seeing it changes.

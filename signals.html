Essay / 03 minute read — 03

Attention
is a currency.

Every feed makes a promise: there’s something here for you. The harder question is what the feed learns to want from you in return.

Signals move fast. A post catches on, a phrase takes hold, a new way of seeing finds its people. Underneath the rush, systems sort and rank what might deserve our next second. Their choices shape what feels popular, urgent, or worth noticing.

But a metric can tell us that a thing was seen without telling us how it landed. It can’t measure the care in a conversation that never goes viral, or the quiet relief of recognizing yourself in a place you didn’t expect to find.

“Being visible and being understood aren’t the same thing.”FIELD NOTE / 003

Listen beyond the numbers

When something starts to trend, look past the count. Who is passing it along? What feeling does it carry? And what quieter signal might be waiting just beyond the scroll?
